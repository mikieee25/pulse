PULSE

Personnel & Unit Lifecycle System for Equipment

A replacement for the office's Excel-based ICT tracker — one place to add, monitor, and retire laptops, tablets, desktops, drones, cameras and printers, with every unit's 3-year lifespan tracked automatically instead of recalculated by hand.

STACK — NEXT.JS · SUPABASE · TAILWIND · ANIMATA.DESIGN

Why replace the spreadsheet

§ 01

The current system is a shared Excel file. It works until two people edit it at once, or until someone has to manually recount which units are due for replacement this year.

Add, edit, delete, and monitor every ICT equipment record from one system

Auto-flag equipment once it passes its 3-year lifespan — no manual recalculation

Restrict assignment to Regular plantilla staff only — never PSS, PES, COS, or Outsourced

Organize every record by division, with scoped access for division-level viewers

Give management a live replacement & budget view, instead of a manually rebuilt yearly sheet

Business rules

§ 04
LIFESPAN

Only Laptop, Tablet, and Desktop carry a 3-year expiry. Drones, cameras, and printers are inventoried but never flagged as expired.

ASSIGNMENT

The "Assigned To" dropdown only lists personnel with plantilla status Regular. PSS, PES, COS, Outsourced, Reserve, and For Transfer never appear in it — enforced at the database level, not just in the form.

PRINTERS

Printers default to division-level ownership, matching how the old sheet tracked them — no individual custodian required.

RETIREMENT

Deleting is destructive and Admin-only. Retiring a unit is the default way to take it out of service while keeping its history.

ACCESS

Admin gets full CRUD across all divisions. Viewer is read-only, and can be scoped to a single division.

Screens

§ 05

Dashboard

KPI cards, division breakdown, and a live "replacement plan for this year" — the automatic version of the old Sheet1.

Equipment list

Per-category tabs, search, filters, and the "+ Add Equipment" flow with the Regular-only assignment dropdown.

Equipment detail

Full record plus a custody timeline pulled from assignment history.

Personnel

Everyone from the roster, with a "+ Add Employee" quick-add for name, division, position, and initials.

Divisions

Per-division rollup: headcount, equipment count, expired count.

Reports

Export current inventory or the replacement plan to Excel, for continuity with how the office already shares this data.

Admin · Users

Manage system logins, roles, and division scoping.

Budget planning

Division × category grid of units due for replacement, priced against editable per-year unit costs.
